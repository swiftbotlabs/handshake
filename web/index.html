Post a bounty. Walk away.

Pick a template, set the reward and a deadline, and sign once. Reactive Network pays the first address the chain proves did the work, or refunds you at the deadline. Nobody has to watch it.

Proof: JobRun(jobId, keeper, work) on Ethereum Sepolia, contract . The keeper is paid.

Proof: Deposit(dst, wad) from the public WETH9 contract on Ethereum Sepolia. Whoever wraps first is paid, even a stranger.

Stops front-running: if a bot copies the claimant's transaction, the proof names the bot and Handshake ignores it.
